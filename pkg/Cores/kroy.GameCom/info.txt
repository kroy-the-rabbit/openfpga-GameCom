Game.com tech demo - highly alpha

Duke Nukem 3D: played on Pocket; controls and sound worked.
BIOS startup matched reference footage.
Duke felt chunky; we weren't impressed.

BIOS: model 71-516, 262144 bytes, CRC32 e235a589.
Assets/gamecom/common/gamecom_bios.bin
ROMs: individual .tgc files in the same folder.
ROM/BIOS libraries are not included.

D-pad: directions. A/B/X/Y: A/B/C/D. Start: Pause. Select: Menu. LT: Sound.
Hold RT to show the stylus; RT + D-pad moves it; RT + A touches.
No cartridges, persistent saves, save states or sleep.

Machine: Jamie Blanks / kitrinx and GameCom_MiSTer contributors.
Pocket integration: kroy, based on pocket-gba.
APF: Analogue. SDRAM reference: Sorgelig. IP: Intel/Altera.
Source and rebuild instructions are available separately on GitHub.
